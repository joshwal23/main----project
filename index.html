About the Squat

Understanding why and how to squat is the first step toward physical dominance. It’s a functional movement pattern that translates to every aspect of life.

flex_direction

Mobility & Longevity

Regular squatting improves joint health in the hips, knees, and ankles, promoting better balance and reducing the risk of injury as you age.

fitness_center

Total Muscle Building

While famous for building quads and glutes, squats trigger a systemic hormonal response that aids muscle growth across the entire body.

sports_gymnastics

Core Strength

Squatting forces your midsection to work overtime to stabilize the spine, resulting in a stronger core than many traditional ab exercises.

menu_book Proper Technique Fundamentals

  • 01. The Stance: Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out. Weight centered over mid-foot.
  • 02. The Descent: Hinge at the hips first, then bend knees. Keep your chest up and core braced throughout.
  • 03. The Depth: Aim for thighs parallel to the floor or lower (the "crease of hip" below the knee).
  • 04. The Drive: Push through the floor with your whole foot, keeping knees in line with toes.
